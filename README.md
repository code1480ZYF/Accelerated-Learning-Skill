---## What This Is

This is a set of three carefully engineered prompt templates that turn any AI assistant into a personalized learning accelerator. Feed them a subject, a textbook, or a book — and get back a comprehensive, well-structured, ready-to-study set of notes.

No more passive reading. No more scattered highlights. Just focused, structured knowledge capture.

## The Three Templates

### Subject Deep-Dive

Master a domain from the ground up.

Combine textbook source material with AI-powered web research to generate hierarchical notes covering first principles, core controversies, layered sub-domains, application scenarios, knowledge graphs, and a full practice question bank.

| Mode | Output | Best For |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Deep** | 100,000+ words | Graduate-level mastery |
| **Standard** | 40,000–60,000 words | Undergraduate study |

## Why You'd Use This

**If you're a student:** Stop re-reading your textbook. Feed it to the template, get a structured study guide with practice questions tailored to your depth level.

**If you're a professional:** Need to ramp up on a new domain fast? The Express mode gives you a working framework in minutes. The Deep mode gives you the full picture.

**If you're a lifelong learner:** Reading a dense book? The Reading Notes template captures the essence, the arguments, and the counterarguments — so you retain more.

---

## How It Works

Each template guides your AI through a structured two-phase workflow:

1. **Setup** — Choose your persona (the "tone" of the output) and your depth level. Provide source material if needed.
2. **Generation** — The AI combines your source material with live web research and produces structured notes following a proven output architecture.

The prompts enforce plain-speak `[explanations]` for abstract concepts and `{visual descriptions}` where helpful — so the output is both rigorous and readable.

## Quick Start

1. Open any AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.).
2. Copy the prompt from one of the template files.
3. Paste it into the AI, follow its instructions, and get your notes.

That's it. No setup, no installation, no configuration.

> **Tip:** Choose your persona and mode before you start. The quality of the output depends on how well these match your goal.

## What People Often Ask

**Which AI should I use?** These templates work with any capable AI assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and others. The output quality depends on the AI's reasoning ability, not the template.

**Do I need to provide source material?** Only for the Subject Deep-Dive (Deep/Standard modes) and Reading Notes templates. The Science Preview template works entirely from web research.

**How long does it take?** Express/Skim modes produce results in one session. Deep mode (100,000+ words) may require multiple rounds of "continue" prompting.

**Can I customize the templates?** Absolutely. They're plain markdown files. Adjust word counts, add output sections, change personas — whatever fits your workflow.
